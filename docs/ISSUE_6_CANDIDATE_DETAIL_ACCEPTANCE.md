## Final acceptance criteria for #6

1. **Roster and route invariant.** Exactly eight candidate detail URLs (`/kandidati/{stable-id}/`) map one-to-one to the *current corrected official municipal list*. Names and list order agree with that record; list sequence is not described as a ballot-paper number without ballot evidence. Each URL loads directly and after refresh, not only after in-app navigation. A detail page has the correct candidate heading/title and a route back to the complete homepage list.
2. **Whole-card navigation.** Clicking or tapping **any non-interactive part** of each homepage candidate card—including portrait/placeholder, list-number area, name, and card background—opens that card's corresponding detail page. Use one native link target per card (prefer an anchor wrapping its non-interactive content); do not create nested links or a JavaScript-only click handler. Its accessible name identifies the candidate and destination. Keyboard Tab reaches each card once in official-list order; Enter activates it; focus is visible. Pointer, touch, keyboard, no-JavaScript, and direct-URL paths agree. Other distinct actions, if ever added, must remain separately operable without hijacking the profile link.
3. **Equal page structure.** All eight pages show the same headings, field order, source/date treatment, empty-state wording, editorial limits, and neutral navigation. Content differences may reflect evidence, not different templates or research effort. Missing/inaccessible evidence is labeled as such, never rendered as proof of absence or refusal.
4. **Field-level provenance.** Official registration and occupation are sourced to the corrected municipal record; candidate-reported experience/programme is explicitly attributed; prior-term public roles require appropriate official/primary records; channels need an identity/self-identification basis; FinStat roles need a direct record, IČO, role/period, and identity bridge; qualifying interviews need a direct URL, publisher, dates, and participation basis. Each public assertion has an adjacent or unambiguously scoped source plus source date (or unknown) and last-checked date. Third-party comments do not become candidate positions, and allegations are not presented as facts.
5. **Coverage and review.** A candidate-by-field private ledger records comparable search methods, cutoff, material reached, inaccessible items, identity gaps, and evidence status for all eight. The public page accurately discloses partial coverage where applicable. Independently verify official roster, content parity, live source links, mobile layout, keyboard/focus/contrast, and reduced/no-motion reading. Candidate copy, correction route, and release exposure receive human editorial approval before publication; issue closure for a draft implementation does **not** imply permission to merge/deploy.
