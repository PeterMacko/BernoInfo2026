# Issue #20 — council-candidate homepage and detail scope

Status: analyst specification, 19 October 2026. Requirements only: no council facts are verified here, no factual pages are implemented, and this document does not authorize publication or deployment. Read with [project charter](PROJECT_CHARTER.md), [research protocol](RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md), [team contract](AGENT_TEAM.md), [homepage spec](INITIAL_HOMEPAGE_SPEC.md), and [candidate detail spec](CANDIDATE_DETAIL_PAGES_SPEC.md). Issue: https://github.com/PeterMacko/BernoInfo2026/issues/20.

## Scope and content model

- Add a second, clearly separate homepage section after the mayoral section. Use exact Slovak office labels consistently: **Kandidáti na starostu obce** (mayoral candidates) and **Kandidáti na poslancov obecného zastupiteľstva** (council candidates). Use **kandidát/kandidátka na poslanca obecného zastupiteľstva** on a council profile. Avoid the ambiguous standalone “kandidáti” in page titles, navigation, and section headings when the office is material.
- Council roster is a separate role and dataset, never merged into the mayoral eight or its comparison. Include each officially registered council candidate exactly once, using the current official notice and amendments as authority. Display in official-list sequence within each volebného obvodu/constituency and kandidátnej listiny/group where the official record defines them. Do not invent a ranking or imply ballot order absent explicit official evidence. Label source-defined number precisely (e.g. “číslo v zozname”); do not call it a ballot number unless verified as such.
- Council profiles are in scope, with stable URLs `/kandidati-na-poslancov/{stable-id}/` (stable machine IDs independent of display spelling/order). Homepage cards link to the matching detail. Each page has one shared template and source/status treatment. No profile should be fabricated before the official roster and a reliable identity-to-record mapping are verified.
- Neutral, comparable fields for every council candidate: (1) official registered name; (2) constituency and list/affiliation exactly as officially recorded, if applicable; (3) official list identifier/sequence where supplied; (4) occupation only if the official record supplies it; (5) candidate-stated programme/priority summary, only when a directly attributable candidate-owned source exists; (6) candidate-owned public website/social channel, only when identity/ownership is established; (7) “rozhodnutie nebolo doložené v preskúmaných zdrojoch k [date]” for unsupported optional fields. For every assertion record source URL, publisher/author, publication date or “nezistený”, observation/last-checked date, and status (`officially verified` / `candidate states` / `not established`). Do not infer positions from comments, supporters, opponents, or same-name results. Do not include resident personal data or unverified allegations as profile facts.
- Homepage cards should show only official name and necessary neutral roster context (constituency/list and official sequence where needed), with identical visual hierarchy and fields. Portraits are optional and must meet the project-wide identity, rights, credit, crop and parity gate; do not let a portrait determine prominence.

## Naming and navigation

- Homepage document title and primary heading identify the whole site/election, not just one office. Section headings must be “Kandidáti na starostu obce” and “Kandidáti na poslancov obecného zastupiteľstva”. Detail `<title>`, `<h1>`, breadcrumb and back link must name the correct office and person; back links read “Späť na kandidátov na poslancov obecného zastupiteľstva” (council) or the corresponding starosta label (mayoral).
- Keep the established `/kandidati/{id}/` routes for mayoral profiles unchanged. Council routes use the separate prefix above; no collision or role ambiguity. Navigation landmarks/link names distinguish the two lists. Use correct Slovak diacritics in visible copy; slug is stable ASCII ID, not a substitute for the display name.

## Final acceptance criteria

1. **Official roster completeness:** Researchers cite the current official municipal/election roster and amendments, verify the full number of council registrations, constituency/list grouping and ordering, and record checked date. The rendered council roster matches it exactly, with no omission, duplication, unverified addition, or silent stale entry. If official records conflict or are unavailable, roster publication is blocked and the gap is explicit.
2. **Distinct and consistent sections:** Homepage places the council section after the mayoral section. Section labels, page titles, headings, breadcrumbs, links and routes use the office terms above consistently; no council candidate appears in mayoral data or vice versa.
3. **Stable one-to-one routing:** Every council card links via one native anchor to exactly one matching `/kandidati-na-poslancov/{stable-id}/` page. Every profile loads by direct URL and refresh; invalid IDs have a deliberate not-found path. Links/back navigation return to the council list. Mayor routes remain intact.
4. **Neutral parity and provenance:** All council profiles use identical field order, wording for empty states, editorial limits and source/date display. Every displayed candidate-specific assertion has a scoped source and last-checked date. Official facts and attributed candidate statements are distinguishable. Missing evidence means only “not established in reviewed sources,” never refusal, absence, or proof. Homepage cards use equal treatment and non-ranking order.
5. **Comparable research:** A private candidate-by-field ledger records source, method, cutoff, dates reached, evidence status, inaccessible materials and gaps for every registered council candidate. Five supplied Facebook sources and discovered candidate-owned sources are covered using the research protocol; disclose per-source partial coverage and do not claim exhaustive coverage. Equal search effort applies across the council roster. Raw personal comments/transcripts and private-group content are not published.
6. **Accessibility and behavior:** Both rosters and profiles work at mobile and desktop widths, with keyboard-only navigation, visible focus, semantic headings/landmarks, accessible link names, screen-reader-readable status/source text, sufficient contrast, and reduced/disabled motion. Each card is one focus stop; Enter activates it. No-JS fallback keeps official roster names/list navigation available or clearly states the limitation.
7. **Verification and handoff:** Developer records successful build and relevant automated checks (roster count/uniqueness, route mapping, link integrity); reviewer independently verifies roster against official source, all routes, content parity/provenance, source links, keyboard/screen-reader semantics, responsive layout and reduced motion. Review findings are resolved and documented before PR handoff. Human editorial approval precedes public release; PR, preview, or issue completion is not release approval.

## Test cases

- Compare parsed rendered council IDs/names/grouping/order with the versioned transcription of the official current roster; assert equal counts, uniqueness, and exactly one rendered entry per official registration; fail on any mismatch.
- For every council card, assert one native link with expected stable URL and accessible name; visit URL directly, refresh, follow back link, and verify correct candidate/role title. Exercise unknown ID and verify not-found behavior. Repeat checks that all eight existing mayoral routes still resolve to the same mayoral candidates.
- Inspect homepage order and visible Slovak labels; assert all council section heading/navigation/title strings use “poslancov obecného zastupiteľstva” and mayoral strings use “starostu obce”, with correct page-specific names.
- For all council detail pages, compare ordered field keys, source/date elements, empty-state text, and word/item caps; assert no unsupported field is rendered as fact and every displayed assertion links to a source with last-checked date/status.
- Keyboard test from page start through both lists: logical order, one Tab stop per card, visible focus, Enter navigation, no nested interactive controls or pointer-only behavior. Verify screen-reader heading/landmark/link-name structure.
- Check at representative narrow mobile and desktop viewports, with motion enabled and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; reading, navigation, and source access remain usable. Disable JavaScript and inspect fallback.
- Verify the research ledger has an entry/status/gap record for each candidate and each required source, with no exhaustive-coverage wording where coverage is partial.

## Decisions and prerequisites

- **Research prerequisite:** the exact council roster, current amendments, any constituencies, group/list labels, and official ordering are not independently verified by this analyst spec. Researcher must establish them from primary records before implementation/content entry.
- **Profile breadth:** default is the same compact neutral fields above; no individual biographies, campaign claims, or council-position inference. Lead/Peter must approve any extra comparison question/topic globally before research or implementation.
- **Programme summary cap:** recommended default if summaries are collected: one summary per candidate, maximum 70 Slovak words, with source link; use the same cap and collection effort for all. Lead/Peter can set a different global cap before copy drafting.
- **Cards and portraits:** no portrait requirement. If included, apply the same project-wide rights/identity and parity gate to all candidates; unresolved portraits use a uniform placeholder.
- **Correction path and release:** use the project’s approved correction route, date corrections, and obtain human editorial approval. Any preview/public exposure and deployment still require the project lead/Peter’s separate approval.
