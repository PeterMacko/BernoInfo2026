**Use decision:** Peter's asserted permission is the owner's task authorization for reproduction/cropping; it is not independent proof of photo ownership, required credit wording or “Cirbi Cirbus” identity. The prior categorical **“0 of 8 cleared” is withdrawn as stale**. This is an evidence/credit flag for editorial review, not a claim that every photographer/rightsholder or depicted identity is independently verified. Facebook photo pages are source locators, not durable image-file URLs. No images were downloaded/copied or published.

**Method and limits:** Read project charter, research protocol and team contract. Used the signed-in managed `openclaw` browser only to read visible Facebook pages on 17 October; no account interaction. The seven conventional portraits and new Cirbus composite were visually inspected; dimensions/date labels are in the matrix. Facebook showed no upload years for the seven pages and only relative “4 h” for the Cirbus composite. Public web search returned “disabled or no provider.” This remains **partial**: no chronological July–October feed sweep, independent face authentication, photographer inquiry or exhaustive alternate-headshot search. No outreach or publication.
