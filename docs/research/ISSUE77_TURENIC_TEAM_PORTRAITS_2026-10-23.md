# Issue #77 — Turenič team portrait replacement

Observed 23 October 2026. Peter requested the higher-resolution portraits adjacent to the seven named people on [Môj tím](https://www.turenic.sk/moj-tim/) in place of the small municipal-magazine crops. The campaign page supplied the name-to-image association; the faces were visually compared with the previously named magazine medallions in `ISSUE24_PDF_PHOTO_AND_CONNECTION_MAP_2026-10-20.md`. This is a targeted seven-image review, not a renewal of the wider source sweep.

The source filenames contain `ChatGPT-Image`. The portraits visibly resemble the magazine images, but the extent of any generative retouching or synthesis is unknown. Accordingly the site labels these as campaign-site portraits with that uncertainty, not as unmodified documentary photographs. Photographer and original capture dates are not established. Peter authorized use of images from this page; no additional Facebook account interaction occurred.
