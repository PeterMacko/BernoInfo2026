## FinStat company roles: direct-record gate still blocked

On 18 October direct requests to [FinStat home](https://www.finstat.sk/) and its public company-search route returned HTTP **403 Cloudflare “Attention Required”**. The earlier eight-candidate pass documented the same browser blockade. I checked candidate site content for FinStat/ORSR direct links where fetchable (Lakota, Šutka, Turenič; Koštial's site returned 403 to this direct fetch); none supplied a usable direct FinStat company-role URL with IČO and identity bridge. Bing/Google name-result leads and Šutka's campaign description of businesses do not establish legal role or exact entity. **Zero of eight candidates have a cleared direct FinStat role link** in this pass. Status for each: `source_inaccessible` / `not_established`, **not** “no company role.” No same-name result was promoted into an identity assertion. Required remaining evidence: direct role/company record URL, IČO, exact role/period, and a separate candidate-to-entity identity bridge; compare an underlying register if obtained. No company name, personal identifier, or unverified role is recorded as a biographical fact here.
