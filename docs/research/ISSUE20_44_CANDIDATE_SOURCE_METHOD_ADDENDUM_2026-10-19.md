Koštial's accessible site and Turenič's 466 on revisit are availability facts only. Do not construe this as no site/programme.

3. **Video:** at most three ranked cards per candidate, no playback, answer-level checking, channel-owner authentication, platform-wide search or earliest-date endpoint. YouTube matching returned unrelated videos for many names; a result showing another candidate's interview was **not** attached to the queried person.
4. **Editorial gate:** R/G may be used only with the qualifications above; P/V/F are **not a complete parity-cleared comparison**. The 44-row method record supports review of where effort was and was not equal. A release claim that all candidate-owned positions were sought/reviewed would still be unsupported.
