## Private equal-treatment preview

Eight files are mirrored into ignored `.private/portrait-preview-images/` for the local preview. They retain their original pixels; cards share one CSS aspect ratio, with per-candidate framing adjustments, including `object-fit: contain` for Kandráč and Cirbus. Run `bash scripts/private-portrait-preview.sh` to build and serve the local preview. The photo set does **not** pass portrait parity: Cirbus's wide seasonal image appears materially different in scale and setting, and Kandráč's source cuts off hair at the top.
